The long wave channel is approximately **2.4 m** long, **0.12 m** wide, and **0.5 m** high. A wave maker is installed at one end, while the bottom contains sloping topography and several inlet and outlet ports.

## Primary uses

The facility can demonstrate wave propagation, shoaling, breaking, and material transport by finite-amplitude waves. Its inlet system can also be used to create density stratification. Detailed specifications and experiment examples will be added later.
