The mini wave channel was converted from a stratification tank. It is **1.0 m** long, approximately **0.1 m** wide, and **0.6 m** high, and can generate near-harmonic waves using a compact wave maker.

## Key features

- Compact structure for straightforward operation and maintenance;
- Suitable for teaching experiments on wave speed and energy propagation;
- Compatible with dye or lighting methods for flow visualization.
